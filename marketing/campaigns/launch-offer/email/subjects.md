# Subject lines + preheaders

The ad is impersonal by design; the **subject** is where the recipient's own
context lives. The preheader is the grey line shown after the subject — it must
continue the subject, never repeat it, and never be left to default.

## Recommended

> **Subject:** Βιβλιοθήκη {Όνομα} — ο πρώτος χρόνος δωρεάν
> **Preheader:** Πλήρης πρόσβαση για 12 μήνες, χωρίς κάρτα. Μόνο 5 θέσεις.

Names the library, then states the offer. Nothing is teased and nothing is
promised that the ad doesn't deliver in its first screen.

---

## Alternatives

**2. Offer only — no personalisation needed**

> **Subject:** Ο πρώτος χρόνος δωρεάν, για 5 βιβλιοθήκες
> **Preheader:** Κατάλογος, μέλη, δανεισμοί και κρατήσεις σε ένα σύστημα.

The fallback when the library's name isn't in your list.

**3. Duration-led**

> **Subject:** 12 μήνες δωρεάν για τη βιβλιοθήκη σας
> **Preheader:** Πλήρες σύστημα διαχείρισης βιβλιοθήκης, στα ελληνικά.

Mirrors the ad's central line, so the open feels continuous.

> **Never anchor on the full-tier annual price.** An earlier draft led with the
> Municipal price struck through against zero. It fails for a good reason: it
> makes every reader — including a school library that now fits the free tier
> outright — picture that invoice landing twelve months out. The ad now says «πακέτα από 390 € τον χρόνο», which is
> the real entry price for a library of any size.

**4. Product-first**

> **Subject:** Το σύστημα διαχείρισης για ελληνικές βιβλιοθήκες
> **Preheader:** Ο πρώτος χρόνος δωρεάν για τις 5 πρώτες.

Leads with the category rather than the discount. Lower open rate, higher-intent
opens — worth using on the segment you care most about.

---

## Rules

- **Greek sentence case, never ALL CAPS.** «ΔΩΡΕΑΝ!!!» is the fastest route to a
  spam folder and it destroys institutional credibility at the same time.
- **No exclamation marks. No emoji.**
- **Don't open with «Δωρεάν».** Filters weight leading promotional tokens; the
  word reads fine later in the line.
- **Under ~55 characters** so it survives truncation on a phone.
- **Never fake a reply** — no `Re:` or `Fwd:` on first contact.

## Testing, honestly

At 30–50 recipients an A/B test gives you noise, not signal. Pick #1, send the
batch, and judge on **replies and applications** — the ad carries no tracking
pixel, so open rates aren't available and wouldn't tell you much anyway.

If you want a comparison, split by **segment** rather than at random: it produces
an answer you can act on for the next wave.
