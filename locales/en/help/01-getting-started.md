## Welcome

Libriant runs everything your library does — catalog, members, lending,
holds, billing — and adapts to how you actually work. This article is the
30-second version of "where's everything?".

## The dashboard

When you sign in you land on the **dashboard**. It shows at-a-glance counts:

- **Catalog** — how many books are on the shelf.
- **Members** — people who can borrow from you.
- **Active loans** — books currently checked out.
- **Overdue loans** — loans whose due date has passed.
- **Queued holds** — members waiting for a book that's currently lent out.

Below the counts you'll see your **current plan**. If there's a payment
problem we'll surface a yellow banner with a deadline and a one-click way
to fix it.

## The sidebar

Every screen in Libriant lives off the left-hand sidebar:

- **Catalog** — every book and every physical copy of every book.
- **Members** — the people who borrow from you.
- **Loans** — circulation: who has what, when it's due, return it here.
- **Reservations** — the holds queue. Members waiting for popular books.
- **Billing** — your plan, payment methods, and the upgrade catalog.
- **Settings** — data model editor and (soon) loan policies, notifications.
- **Help** — you are here.

## First three things to do

1. **Add your first member.** Even if it's a placeholder — having a real
   row makes the rest of the app feel concrete.
2. **Add your first book.** Type an ISBN and we'll look it up for you, or
   fill in the details by hand.
3. **Check the book out.** Once you have a member and a copy, click
   "New loan" — that's the moment Libriant clicks.

Each one of those is its own article in this Help section if you want a
step-by-step.
