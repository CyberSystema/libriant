## What we are offering

To the **first {{SPOTS_TOTAL}} libraries** who join:

- Full access to Libriant's **{{PLAN_NAME}}** plan, for **{{MONTHS}} months**.
- **At no charge.** No credit card and no payment details. No document is

- Help moving your catalogue across from whatever you use today (CSV, Excel or

- A direct line to us for anything you need.

**That does not mean you will pay {{PLANNED_PRICE}} afterwards.**
{{PLAN_NAME}} is the plan you get free now, so that nothing constrains you. At
the end of the year **you** choose the plan that fits the size of your library.
Plans start at **€390 a year** (or €39 a month), and for smaller collections
(up to 5,000 titles) there is a free plan as well. The {{SPOTS_TOTAL}} founding libraries
also keep a permanent discount.

## What we ask in return

Nothing binding. We will not ask for a public statement, we will not use your
name in any marketing material without your written permission, and we will not
publish anything about your library.

If at some point you want to tell us what could be better, we will listen
carefully — but it is not a condition of the offer.

## What happens after {{MONTHS}} months

**You are never charged automatically.** There is no card on file, so there is
nothing to charge. A month before the end we will get in touch, and you choose
one of the following:

1. **Continue** on a plan of your choosing. Founding libraries keep a permanent
   discount, which we will agree together.
2. **Stop.** You download all of your data in an open format and leave. No
   questions, no charge, no notice period.

If you do not reply at all, the account simply stops being active. It does not
turn into a paid subscription.

## Your data is yours

Your catalogue, your members and your loan history **belong to you, always**.
You can export all of it, at any time, from inside the application and without
asking us. We do not use your data to keep hold of you.

For your members' data, **you are the data controller and we are the
processor**. Before you register your first member we will sign a Data
Processing Agreement that puts this in writing.

## Security and availability

- The system **passed a full security audit** before release and is covered by
  automated tests that run on every change to the code.
- Your data is hosted in the **European Union**, with **daily backups** and an
  off-server copy.
- The service's availability and support terms are agreed with you in writing
  before you start.
- **Exporting all of your data** is available at any moment, in one click,
  without asking us.
