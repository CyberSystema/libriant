# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated: 2026-06-22**

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") forms part of the
[Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and applies to everyone who uses Libriant.

## You must not

1. **Break the law** or use the Service to process data you have no lawful basis
   to process, or in violation of the GDPR or Greek law.
2. **Upload unlawful or harmful content**, including malware, or content that
   infringes others' intellectual-property or privacy rights.
3. **Misuse personal data** — including entering special-category data without a
   lawful basis, or processing members' data for purposes they were not informed
   of.
4. **Attack or probe the Service** — no unauthorised access, scanning,
   penetration testing without prior written permission, credential stuffing, or
   circumventing authentication, rate limits, tenancy isolation, or quotas.
5. **Disrupt the Service** — no denial-of-service, excessive automated load,
   scraping beyond documented APIs, or actions that degrade performance for other
   libraries.
6. **Abuse multi-tenancy** — no attempt to access another library's data or to
   escalate privileges beyond your assigned role.
7. **Resell or sublicense** the Service, or use it to build a competing product,
   except as expressly permitted.
8. **Send spam or abusive messages** through any messaging/email features.
9. **Misrepresent identity** or impersonate others.
